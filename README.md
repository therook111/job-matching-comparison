Graduation Thesis for Spring 2026 Semester - National Economics University (Vietnam).

This project aims to compare the effectiveness of several job matching methodologies in literature, using a synthetic dataset of job descriptions and resumes. The chosen algorithm are:
- [Frazzetto, P., Haq, M. U. U., Fabris, F., & Sperduti, A. (2025). Graph Neural Networks for Candidate-Job Matching: An Inductive Learning Approach. Data Science and Engineering.](https://doi.org/10.1007/s41019-025-00293-y)    
- [Lai, K.-H., Yang, Z.-R., Lai, P.-Y., Wang, C.-D., Guizani, M., & Chen, M. (2024). Knowledge-Aware Explainable Reciprocal Recommendation. Proceedings of the AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence, 38(8), 8636–8644.](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i8.28708)
- [Yu, X., Xu, R., Xue, C., Zhang, J., Ma, X., & Yu, Z. (2025). ConFit v2: Improving Resume-Job Matching using Hypothetical Resume Embedding and Runner-Up Hard-Negative Mining. Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics (ACL 2025).](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12361)
